# Our taxi service

Abingdon Taxi Solutions provides taxi services to customers. Our product
direction is AI-first, with experiences for four groups:

| Role | Customer need | AI capability |
|---|---|---|
| Rider | Get help with a booking | Rider support assistant (OpenAI, in use) |
| Driver | Understand a contract and current earnings | Ask questions through Gemini (proposed) |
| Manager | Review productivity and absence | Explain dashboard trends (proposed) |
| Owner | Understand fleet performance | Summarize the fleet overview (proposed) |

The repository records the rider support model that is already in use.
The `workshop/` files are example inputs to introduce one change at a time,
not evidence that a service is running. No application, dashboards,
employee records, runtime service, or credentials are included. Trust
must verify any real deployment before approving its claims.
